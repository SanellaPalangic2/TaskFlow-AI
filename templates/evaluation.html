{{ ui.icon('info', 'icon-sm') }}How results are decided

Three checks per case, computed in Python:

  • Classification: the model's intent is one of the accepted intents.
  • Important information: every expected item appears in the model's missing information or concerns, and nothing on the "must not claim" list is reported missing.
  • Action category: the suggested team is one of the accepted teams.

Passed all three pass · Review required one does not · Failed two or more do not, or the API call failed.

The model never grades itself.
